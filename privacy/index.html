Spin!

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Spin! is an app for logging the records you listen to. This page explains what data it handles, where that data goes, and how to delete it.

  • Your records, spins and settings stay on your device, unless you sign in to sync them.
  • Signing in is optional. Everything works without an account.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and your data is never sold.
  • The app sends anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings → Privacy.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who is responsible

Spin! is made by an independent developer in Greece, who is the data controller for the data described here. For any question or request about your data, including who the developer is, email spin.app@outlook.com.

Data that stays on your device

Your records, artists, spins, ratings, notes, settings, earned badges and any covers you add are stored inside the app on your device. They aren't sent anywhere unless you sign in (see Spin! account) or save a backup file yourself.

Backup files are saved wherever you choose (your device's storage, Google Drive, and so on). Spin! doesn't keep a copy.

Reminders, such as the evening streak reminder and "time to flip the record", are scheduled on your device. No server sends them.

Discogs (optional)

If you connect your Discogs account, the app talks to Discogs directly from your device. Your Discogs login is stored only on that device. Spin! reads your collection and, when you choose, adds or removes records and updates ratings on Discogs. Album details, prices and cover images are loaded from Discogs' servers. Discogs' own handling of your data is described in the Discogs privacy policy.

MusicBrainz

To find an album's running time, the app may look it up on MusicBrainz by its barcode, title and artist. No information about you is included. As with any website, MusicBrainz sees your device's IP address when the app connects.

Spin! account (optional)

You can sign in to back up your library and sync it across your devices. Signing in with Google gives Spin! your email address and the name and profile picture link on your Google account.

While you're signed in, the account stores:

  • your email address and sign-in details,
  • your records, artists, spins, ratings, notes and app settings,
  • covers you uploaded yourself.

Your Discogs login is never synced. The account is hosted by Supabase, Inc. on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, under Supabase's data processing agreement. Each account can only read and change its own data, and all connections are encrypted.

We use this data only to keep your library backed up and in sync, as part of the service you signed up for. It's kept until you delete it or your account.

Crash reports

When something goes wrong, the app sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), stored in the EU, so bugs can be found and fixed. A report contains:

  • the error and where in the app it happened,
  • the app version, your device model and operating system version,
  • a few recent app events, such as which screen was opened,
  • a random ID created when the app was installed, used to count how many devices an error affects.

Reports don't include your name, email, account, or records. Sentry works out an approximate location (city and country) from your IP address when a report arrives and doesn't store the IP address itself. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.

We send crash reports based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Send crash reports.

Android backup

If you've turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may copy the app's data (your records, spins and settings) to your Google account, so a new device can start with your library. This is Android's own feature, controlled by you and covered by Google's privacy policy. Spin! leaves your Discogs and Spin! account logins out of it.

What Spin! doesn't do

  • No advertising or ad tracking.
  • No analytics, beyond the crash reports above.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone for their own purposes.
  • No access to your contacts, location or files, beyond the files you pick yourself. The camera is used only while you scan a record's barcode, and the picture never leaves your device.

Your rights

Under the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask to see, correct, export or delete the data Spin! holds about you, and object to its use. Most of this you can do in the app: Settings shows your data, Save backup exports all of it, and you can delete your account (see below). For anything else, email spin.app@outlook.com. You'll get an answer within a month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Greece that's the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: in the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, synced records and uploaded covers are deleted from the cloud right away. You can choose whether to keep a copy on the device. You can also request deletion by email.
  • Data on your device: uninstalling Spin!, or clearing its storage in your device's settings, removes everything stored on that device.
  • Crash reports: these aren't linked to you, and they're deleted automatically after 90 days.

Children

Spin! isn't directed at children under 15, and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app.
