Delete your Spin! account

Spin! · spin.app@outlook.com

A Spin! account is optional: it backs up your library and syncs it across your devices. You can delete it, and everything stored in it, in either of these ways.

In the app

  1. Open Spin! and tap the gear icon on Home to open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Choose whether to keep a copy of your records on this device, to keep using Spin! without an account.

The deletion happens right away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email spin.app@outlook.com from the email address of your account (for a Google sign-in, your Google address), with the subject "Delete my Spin! account". Your account will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply once it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your account: email address and sign-in details.
  • Everything synced to it: records, artists, spins, ratings, notes and app settings.
  • Covers you uploaded.

Nothing from the account is kept afterwards.

What isn't affected

  • Records kept on a device, if you chose to keep a copy, stay on that device until you remove them or uninstall the app.
  • Anonymous crash reports aren't linked to your account, and they're deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Backup files you saved yourself stay wherever you saved them.
  • Your Discogs account and collection aren't touched.
